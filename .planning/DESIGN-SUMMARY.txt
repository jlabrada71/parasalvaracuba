================================================================================
DESIGN SYSTEM PRINCIPLES APPLIED
================================================================================

1. NO-LINE RULE
   - No 1px solid borders for sectioning
   - Use background color shifts and surface tones instead

2. SURFACE HIERARCHY & NESTING
   - Treat UI like stacked sheets of paper
   - Cards (surface-container-lowest) sit on light backgrounds
   - Natural lift through layering, not drop shadows

3. GLASS & GRADIENT RULE
   - Use tonal approaches for glassmorphism effects
   - Primary CTAs: gradient from primary to primary-container at 135°

4. AMBIENT SHADOWS ONLY
   - 24px blur, 6% opacity, on-surface tint
   - Used sparingly for hover states

5. EDITORIAL AESTHETIC
   - Asymmetric layouts (8.5rem left, 5.5rem right margins)
   - Generous whitespace
   - High-contrast typography
   - Professional, curated feel
